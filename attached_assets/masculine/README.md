# Masculine Collection Logos

Sword, eagle, and brotherhood crests for the Masculine Collection (`/masculine`)
and the "Masculine Collection" filter in logo pickers.

**Male premium polos:** the eight shiny eagle-and-swords colorways
(Black & White, Navy & Gold, Purple & Gold, Brown & Gold, Red & Gold,
Burgundy & Gold, Silver & Gold, All Gold — logo IDs #120–#127) are the emblems
for men's premium polo shirts. Canonical copies + notes live in
`attached_assets/male-polo-shirts/`. Polos ship fixed — left chest, right sleeve,
and inside tag — with no placement customization.

Upload new masculine logos into this folder, then register them in
`client/src/lib/logoCatalog.ts` under `MASCULINE_LOGO_IDS` / `allLogos`.
