# Football Teams Sports Edition

Seasonal Khomplete Khemistri football crest artwork for the August–February
collection. The 28 uploaded designs are registered as logo IDs `400`–`427` in
`client/src/lib/logoCatalog.ts` and can be selected on customizable products.

The uploaded Raiders artwork reads “Oakland Raiders,” so the storefront labels
it as the “Raiders — Legacy Oakland Edition” rather than presenting it as a
current Las Vegas design.

When adding another team:

1. Put its image in this folder with a descriptive lowercase filename.
2. Add its metadata to `shared/footballTeams.ts`.
3. Import and map the image in `client/src/lib/logoCatalog.ts`.

Team names and marks belong to their respective owners. Only publish or sell
artwork that the business is authorized to use.
